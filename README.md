
#  LifeSci360 – Clinical Research & Laboratory Management Platform

LifeSci360 is a full-stack Clinical Research and Laboratory Management Platform developed using **ASP.NET Core**, **Angular**, and **Microsoft SQL Server**. It is designed to streamline clinical research operations by providing a centralized system for managing study protocols, patient enrollment, laboratory workflows, sample tracking, compliance monitoring, and reporting. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The platform enables research organizations, laboratories, and clinical trial teams to efficiently manage the complete lifecycle of clinical studies through an intuitive web interface. It offers secure role-based access, real-time data management, and centralized storage of research information, helping improve operational efficiency while ensuring regulatory compliance. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Key Features

-  Secure authentication with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
-  Clinical study and protocol management
-  Patient enrollment and visit scheduling
-  Laboratory sample registration and tracking
-  Laboratory test result management
-  Interactive dashboards and analytics
-  Compliance reporting and audit logs
-  Real-time notifications and alerts
-  User and role management
-  Responsive and user-friendly interface

LifeSci360 is built with a scalable, modular architecture, making it suitable for research laboratories, pharmaceutical companies, and Contract Research Organizations (CROs) seeking a secure and efficient solution for clinical trial and laboratory management.
